# Comparing two Runs

You changed something and you want to know whether it worked. `nunatak
compare` puts two Runs side by side, one row per function, and says of
every difference whether it is larger than the noise it was measured
through.

The recipe comes first. The page after it says what a row is, why some
functions never appear in the diff, and what `significant` is arithmetic
for.
