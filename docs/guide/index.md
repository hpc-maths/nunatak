# User guide

For whoever profiles an application: how to get what you need out of
nunatak, and why it behaves as it does.

The workflows come first because they are what a reader arrives with: a
job to submit, a CI to gate, a site that lets nothing out. The page after
them teaches the vocabulary every other page uses - what an estimated
value is, what an unresolved Hotspot is, and why a missing capability is
named rather than fatal.
